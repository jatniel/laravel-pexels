# Changelog

All notable changes to `laravel-pexels` will be documented in this file.

## First stable release. - 2026-09-27

- Search photos with filters (orientation, size, color, locale)
- Curated photos, photo by ID, random photo
- Collections and collection photos
- Paginated results (LengthAwarePaginator)
- Local downloads to any disk (streamed, sync or queued)
- Blade components: <x-pexels-image> and <x-pexels-background>

- Response caching, local rate limiting, timeout and retries
- Supports PHP 8.3+ and Laravel 11, 12 and 13
